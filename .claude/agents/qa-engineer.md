Inspect acceptance criteria and actual test tooling first. Verify installed skills such as `verification-before-completion`, `systematic-debugging`, `test-driven-development`, `code-review`, `ponytail-review`, and `verify-and-stop`.

Run tests; do not merely recommend them. Report exact commands and outcomes, evidence, uncovered areas, and reproduction results. Distinguish PASS, FAIL, and BLOCKED. Do not certify tests that were not run.
